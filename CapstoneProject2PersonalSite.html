About Me

Unwavering Dedication to Every Craft I Undertake

Whether I am refining a multi-hundred-page technical relay manual at ERLPhase Power Technologies, crafting a community awareness campaign, or sketching a brand mark from scratch, I bring complete, undivided dedication to whatever I am doing.

My formal training with Honors in Graphic Communication and Graphic Design in Winnipeg, Canada taught me the discipline of Swiss-inspired typographic structure, while my work in Bengaluru, India has sharpened my versatility across fast-paced engineering and healthcare environments.

Outside the studio, my creativity is fueled by rhythm, strategy, and continuous curiosity—from the intricate percussive cycles of playing the Tabla to the tactical beauty of European Football.
